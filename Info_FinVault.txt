Step 3. Important database rules
These are essential for a financial application:
1. Double-entry accounting for every financial transaction.
2. Never directly modify completed ledger entries.
3. Use database transactions for debit and credit operations.
4. Idempotency to prevent duplicate payments.
5. Maintain complete audit history.
6. Use decimal data types for monetary values.
7. Use concurrency control to prevent incorrect balances.
